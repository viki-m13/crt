# Economic edge first / net Sharpe 3 — experiment registration

Registered 2026-09-09 before computing this batch's market outcomes. Base CRT b8d26fb3f0382427b580cfa403cc5708a147df05. Research only. Main, production, other repositories and Gridpull unchanged. The objective has changed from 95% endpoint precision to >=3 annualized net excess-return Sharpe on the complete out-of-sample equity curve. These are different targets. No global priority/never-before-done claim is justified without a prior-art study.

## Economic hypotheses, not confidence-score inventions

H1 INFORMATION / LIQUIDITY SEPARATION: common price information diffuses across economically related stocks while transient, stock-specific inventory pressure can reverse. Fit a past-only latent covariance model, remove its common component and separate recent innovations from slow residual trend. A disagreement score fades short residual shocks only when the slow residual state is not corroborating permanent deterioration. The proposed increment is the interaction and selection rule, not the established idea of statistical arbitrage. Hedge the same common-risk exposures in actual holdings, rather than claiming that a residual return is directly investable.

H2 CASH-FLOW TWIN DISLOCATION: two publicly traded equity classes of the same business share much economic exposure, while voting rights, liquidity, index inclusion and clientele cause a time-varying premium. Trade departures from a past robust premium baseline, with a slow-trend veto. Prices alone do not prove equal legal rights; this is conditional research, not an arbitrage guarantee. Fixed candidate pairs: GOOG/GOOGL (not before 2014-04-03); FOX/FOXA (2019-03-19); NWS/NWSA (2013-07-01); Z/ZG (2015-08-17); UA/UAA (2016-04-08); DISCA/DISCK (2008-09-18); LBTYA/LBTYK (2005-09-08); BRK-A/BRK.B (2000-01-03 or later usable history). Never rank pairs using full-history outcomes. Present-day selection of this limited pair list is a coverage/selection limitation, explicitly not a full PIT security-master universe.

H3 RESET/TRACKING FRICTION: leveraged-ETF daily exposure and actual hedge funding imply a precise self-financing accounting test. First falsify the alleged free variance-carry identity in prior CRT research: a linear combination of log returns is NOT a portfolio's arithmetic P&L. Then test whether lagged tracking dislocations, not raw volatility drag, predict subsequent implementable hedged returns. Fixed pairs TQQQ/QQQ (3), UPRO/SPY (3), SPXL/SPY (3), SSO/SPY (2), TNA/IWM (3), URTY/IWM (3). SOXL/SMH is deliberately excluded because different benchmark exposures cannot be declared algebraically neutral.

## Frozen initial mechanisms

For H1: rolling 252-session covariance on past eligible members, standardized returns, first five principal directions (no future fit); refresh every 21 sessions. Signal features: residual sums over 5, 21 and 126 sessions and a slow-trend disagreement interaction. Compare plain 5-session reversal, 21-session reversal, slow residual momentum (126 minus 21), disagreement-gated 5-session reversal, and consensus of the two reversal scales. Weights are projected away from the current factor loadings and dollar exposure, then scaled to gross <=1, with no leverage optimization. A long-only residual-momentum control is retained. All eigenvectors, eligibility and weights use information available by signal close.

For H2: premium median/std on 126 prior observations, recent premium change over 5 sessions, and 63-session premium drift. Compare plain mean reversion; mean reversion after a reversal in the premium's recent motion; mean reversion vetoed when slow drift supports continued divergence. Require >=126 actual common observations. Threshold |z|>=1.5 fixed; no post-result optimization.

For H3: compute daily arithmetic tracking error, its lagged 5-session sum minus trailing 63-session mean, and compare constant hedge, mean-reverting tracking error and spread divided by trailing error scale. Synthetic exact-tracking ETFs are negative controls; a self-financing daily hedge must earn zero gross before fees in that fixture, regardless of realized variance. A static-share hedge is not continuously beta-neutral and must be marked to market as such.

Primary compatibility track retains the earlier minimum 30-session horizon via staggered, locked 30/60-session lots. Any 1/5-session turnover probe is explicitly a diagnostic and cannot satisfy that earlier constraint. Signal at close t; entries at close t+1 or later. No same-close execution based on that close. Planned entry/exit is recorded, and all open positions are marked daily, including underwater and immature lots. No selecting exits after seeing prices. At most 1x gross at inception, fixed book notional divided across staggered cohorts; excess gross from drift is reported, not silently ignored.

## Financing and data

Use the immutable public CRT inputs already downloaded; size and SHA-256 verified. Actual NYSE session calendar. Historical index membership, not current names, defines H1 eligibility. Prices are adjusted/mixed and terminal corporate actions incomplete: do not claim a fully bias-free tradable strategy. H2/3 use explicit fixed instrument sets with inception restrictions. Missing held prices are not silently deleted; report stale-mark days and a conservative adverse-liquidation sensitivity. Price errors are not retrospectively clipped out of returns.

Primary cost assumptions: 5bp per traded dollar per side, 3% annualized short borrow, no interest credit on restricted short proceeds, and financing at risk-free plus 2% for a cash deficit. Credit free cash at observed RF when available; otherwise explicitly use a fixed 3% RF scenario, with 0/5% sensitivities. Subtract RF in Sharpe, never advertise cash yield as alpha. ETF embedded expenses already in prices are not subtracted twice. Actual historical borrow quotes/availability are absent; 1% and 10% borrow sensitivities and 10bp slippage stress are mandatory. Costs are scenarios, not broker-verified fills.

## Research / evaluation boundaries

Development through 2015; selection/validation 2016–2020; final model-relative test 2021–archive cutoff, disclosed as previously researched history, NOT a virgin project-wide holdout. For the shorter available NDX PIT window, development 2015–2018, validation 2019–2020, evaluation 2021+. Freeze the best eligible primary configuration using validation net Sharpe only before examining test metrics; also publish all fixed candidates. Additional invention is allowed only as a separately timestamped batch, with prior test exposure disclosed.

Use every daily account return (including zero-exposure dates) for excess Sharpe; annualization sqrt(252); also report autocorrelation-adjusted long-run variance Sharpe, CAGR, drawdown from initial equity, worst daily/monthly loss, turnover, average/max gross, short exposure, missing-price events and P&L concentration. Report calendar years, subperiods, leave-one-pair/group contributions, shuffled-signal / sign-reversed controls, and time-block bootstrap confidence intervals. Multiple-testing and small-n are not solved by ordinary bootstrap intervals. No claiming independent trials from overlapping holds, correlated pairs or millions of rows.

## Acceptance

Economic mechanism passes implementable P&L and negative controls; net test Sharpe >=3 on a nontrivial multi-year full curve; no accounting/forward-data defects; reasonable costs, concentration and adverse scenarios; matched control evidence; and separate prospective execution/borrow confirmation before deployment. Hitting 3 on an in-sample segment, log-spread pseudo-P&L, synthetic returns, or a fast diagnostic does not fulfill the mandate. Record failed and successful experiments, not only the winner. This protocol is a plan, not a claim of an edge already established.
