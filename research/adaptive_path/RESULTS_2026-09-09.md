# Adaptive Path — first results (2026-09-09)

The first broad price-only MEAP model **failed** the 95% objective. That rejects this configuration, not the price-only research program.

PIT S&P test, chronological prequential fits, evaluation 2013+, missing matured outcomes counted as failures:

| horizon | all-stock positive rate | best meaningful selected precision | max calibrated p |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 sessions | 57.7% | 63.4% at p>=0.60 (23,255 rows) | 71.5% |
| 60 sessions | 59.7% | 62.6% at p>=0.70 (2,960 rows) | 85.8% |
| 126 sessions | 62.3% | 79.3% at p>=0.80 (786 rows) | 89.0% |
| 252 sessions | 64.7% | 73.9% at p>=0.80 (1,677 rows) | 85.4% |

No horizon produced a 90% probability bucket, let alone a validated 95% bucket. The 30-session edge also deteriorated materially in 2022-26. Path centers did not beat a flat-price path on mean log-MAE, so the first trajectory model is rejected.

## Decision

**Do not kill price-only research. Kill only generic classifier tuning.** Run two independent programs in parallel:

1. **AHE — Adaptive Hard-Evidence Horizon**: event/filing structure + market confirmation.
2. **SIEVE — Sequential Invariance and Extreme-Event Veto Ensemble**: price/volume-only selective forecasting designed around finding tiny, stable regions of state space rather than estimating direction for every stock.

SIEVE is allowed to abstain almost always. It searches for causal state predicates that survive era, ticker, sector and regime perturbations; uses negative controls and adversarial vetoes; chooses 30/60/90/126/180/252 sessions only from matured prior outcomes; and requires the same predicate to remain >95% under pessimistic missing-outcome treatment and date clustering. Discovery/calibration and final evaluation remain separated. No post-hoc extension of a losing horizon.

Additional experiment families remain explicitly in scope: cross-sectional rank transitions, volatility-surface proxies available from price data, drawdown-recovery geometry, multi-timescale trend phase, residual momentum after market/sector neutralization, breadth/dispersion interactions, change-point/regime age, tail-event exclusion, ensemble disagreement as an abstention signal, conformal/selective risk control, nearest-neighbor state recurrence with matched random controls, and combinations of these. A failed family is documented rather than silently discarded.

## AHE

AHE only issues a forecast when a stock has both:

1. **Structural evidence** whose payoff clock is knowable ex ante (material contract / definitive transaction / earnings-information shock / capital-return event / other filing-defined catalyst), and
2. **Market confirmation** from the PIT price panel (relative strength, breadth/regime, volatility and crowding vetoes).

Each catalyst family owns an allowed horizon set >=30 sessions. The chosen horizon is the earliest horizon whose *matured historical cohort*, available at that issue date, clears the 95% precision gate after multiplicity adjustment. If none clears it, abstain. A losing forecast can never have its deadline extended.

## Validation requirements

- PIT universe and information available by issue timestamp only.
- Candidate definitions frozen before outcome calculation for confirmatory tests.
- 30/60/90/126/180/252-session endpoints tested separately.
- Same-date and same-event clustering; no treating correlated ticker rows as independent.
- Threshold/horizon chosen only from matured past cohorts.
- Final holdout/prospective segment untouched by design selection.
- Report coverage as well as precision; zero picks is not 100% accuracy.
- Continue inventing and testing new families until the evidence frontier is mapped; never convert an exploratory >95% result into a validated claim without a fresh confirmatory test.
